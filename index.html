How to read the card

  • Days left Countdown from your view date to the public enroll-by: Sep 30 2026 11:59 p.m. ET. Color is secondary; the number (or TODAY / CLOSED) carries meaning.
  • Status chip Text-labeled: already on autopay → no extra action for the temporary 1% reduction vs not on autopay → enroll via servicer vs unsure → check servicer. StudentAid.gov is not the autopay toggle.
  • Eligibility Optional Direct Loan originated-after-Jul 1 2012 flag. If unknown, the card says eligibility is not confirmed. We never invent that you qualify.
  • Benefit footnote ED: reduction through June 30, 2028 if enrolled by Sep 30 (or already enrolled). Baseline autopay reduction was 0.25%; already-enrolled borrowers get an additional 0.75% automatically (total 1%) — ED Jun 18 2026 public framing, not an estimate for your loan.
  • No invented savings. Business Insider (Sep 1 2026) published an example (~$23/mo for a graduate borrower, $50k @ 7.94%). That is their example — it does not appear as your number on this card.
  • Action line Enroll in autopay in your loan servicer account; details at the StudentAid.gov auto-pay interest-rate-reduction page. Not plan advice.
